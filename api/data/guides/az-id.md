Arizona State ID After Release

- Wait at least 24 hours after release to ensure records sync.
- Gather documents: DOC release papers, Social Security card (or proof), and two proofs of AZ residency (mail, shelter letter, or lease).
- If you lack an SSN card, you can still apply with a completed SS-5 receipt or SSA printout.
- Visit an Arizona MVD office or authorized third-party provider. Check hours online.
- Fees: Expect ~$12–$25. Fee waivers may be available for reentry programs — ask staff.
- If you don’t have a birth certificate, request a certified copy from your birth state vital records.
- Ask for a temporary paper credential the same day; physical card arrives by mail.
- Keep your mailing address stable for 2–3 weeks; ask a shelter or friend to receive mail if needed.
- If you cannot travel, ask your reentry case manager about mobile or outreach ID events.
- Keep receipts and take photos of documents for your records.
